Research Project 2026

OpenAI Whisper Multilingual Performance

  • ASR
  • Multilingual
  • Tokenization

Overview

For a term project, I analyzed the performance of pre-trained transformer-based ASR models (e.g., Whisper) and other neural ASR models across multilingual settings. I observed cross-lingual interference effects in shared-script languages (Hindi, Marathi, Nepali) and conducted linguistic error analysis accordingly.
