LLM Evaluation · Social Impact of Computing

I study how language shapes the behavior and impact of AI systems.

Hi, I’m Aditi. I study how language models respond to sensitive prompts, encode social and political framing, and perform across multilingual settings. I’m especially interested in evaluation research that reveals systemic bias, ecosystem-level risk, and gaps in who language technologies serve.

I am a master’s student in Computational Linguistics at the University of Washington, with an undergraduate degree in Statistics and Economics from the University of California, Santa Barbara. My work spans themes of LLM use as mental health aids, slant analysis in prompts, political framing in journalism, and language inclusivity in speech models.

01

Research

Human-centered AI evaluation and safety

I develop ways to evaluate AI systems around real human needs, especially where conventional accuracy metrics miss consequential behavior, uncertainty, or downstream harm.

Psychological safety in language models

I study benchmarks, user simulations, and multi-turn evaluation methods for mental-health and therapy-adjacent interactions, with an emphasis on transparent definitions of risk and meaningful clinical involvement.

Multilingual speech and NLP

I investigate how speech systems behave across languages and resource conditions, including token-level uncertainty and decoding behavior in Hindi, Marathi, Nepali, and other multilingual settings.
