LLM Evaluation · Social Impact of Computing

My interests include latent state world modeling, evaluating LLMs for safe interaction in mental health contexts, and NLP research that exposes systemic bias.

I am a master’s student in Computational Linguistics at the University of Washington, Seattle with an undergraduate degree in Statistics and Economics from the University of California, Santa Barbara.

My work spans behavioral state prediction via speech and text data, AI fairness, evaluation design, and language model bias in multimodal and multilingual settings. I have always been drawn to the social impact of computing, and I hope to carry this forward in my research career.
