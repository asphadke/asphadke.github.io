Aditi Phadke

I am a master’s student at the University of Washington, Seattle studying Computational Linguistics, advised by Gina-Anne Levow and Fei Xia. This summer, I worked in Prof. Tim Althoff’s Behavioral Data Science Lab as a Graduate Student Research Assistant.

I have always been drawn to the social impact of large language modeling, and I hope to carry this forward in my research career. My interests include multi-turn LLM evaluation, safe LLM-human interaction in mental health contexts, mental state world modeling, and NLP research that exposes systemic bias. Language is my first love, so I hope to extend my research to multilingual settings in the future.

A qualitative study of the field and recommendations on concrete challenges seen in building AI evaluation tools for mental health, as experienced by 20 mental health experts, policy researchers, and mental health benchmark developers in the domain.

A literature review of LLM evaluation methods for mental health, including a comparison of static benchmarking methods and user simulation-based evaluation methods.

Ongoing Research Fall 2026

Behavioral state world modeling Fall 2026

Predicting the unobservable behavioral state of the user from observable metrics such as audio and text. This is an extension of an ARPA-H grant project that constitutes similar work with wearable health sensor and location data.

  • User state simulation

Research Project 2026

OpenAI Whisper Multilingual Performance

Analyzed the performance of OpenAI Whisper across multilingual settings, observing cross-lingual interference effects in the shared-script languages of Hindi, Marathi, Nepali. I conducted linguistic error analysis accordingly.

  • ASR
  • Multilingual
  • Tokenization
